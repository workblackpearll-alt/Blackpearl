Celebrations on a Private Yacht in Mumbai

One yacht, one booking at a time, up to 100 guests. Choose your occasion to see ideas, prices and timings.

Yacht Proposals Ask the question on open water, with Mumbai's skyline turning gold behind you and nobody else on board but the people you choose. Birthday Parties Turn a milestone birthday into a night at sea. Lady Alice hosts up to 100 guests across her open decks, with the whole yacht reserved for your party alone. Corporate Events Take the team, your clients or a product launch out of the conference room. Lady Alice gives you a private venue on the water for up to 100 guests. Anniversaries & Dinners Celebrate the years together with a candlelit table on deck, the sea around you and the city lights in the distance. Engagement Ceremonies Bring both families together on the water. Exchange rings under a floral setup on deck, then celebrate with dinner and music as the sun goes down. Bachelor & Bachelorette Give the bride or groom a send-off at sea. Music on deck, drinks with a view and the whole yacht for your group. Pre-Wedding Shoots Shoot on open decks, the bow and a styled master suite, with the sea and Mumbai's skyline as your backdrop. Private Parties Cocktail evenings, DJ nights and celebrations for no reason at all. Lady Alice gives you a private party venue on the water for up to 100 guests. Family Celebrations Reunions, parents' anniversaries, homecomings and festivals: bring every generation together on the water for a day they will talk about for years. Harbour Sailing Cruises Sail out of Princess Ferry Wharf into the quieter waters on the outskirts of Mumbai harbour, then drop anchor and enjoy the view.
