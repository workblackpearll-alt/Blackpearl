Corporate Events on a Private Yacht in Mumbai

Take the team, your clients or a product launch out of the conference room. Lady Alice gives you a private venue on the water for up to 100 guests.

Use the morning slot for a leadership offsite or the evening for a gala dinner or launch. Our team plans the run of show, catering and AV with you.

What you get

  • Leadership offsites, town halls, client dinners and team celebrations
  • Brand launches and showcases with space for a stage or runway
  • Morning (6 am – 2 pm) and evening (2 pm – 11 pm) slots
  • One booking at a time, so your guests never share the yacht

Timings and pricing

Offsites usually take a 5-hour day charter; galas and launches suit the evening. Charters run in a day slot (6 am – 2 pm) or an evening slot (2 pm – 11 pm), for 3, 5 or 6 hours. 3 hours: 1 hr sailing · 2 hrs at anchor · 5 hours: 1.5 hrs sailing · 3.5 hrs at anchor · 6 hours: 3 hrs sailing · 3 hrs at anchor.

Every event is different, so we quote each charter personally. Send your request and our team shares pricing for your date, slot and plans on WhatsApp.

Where you board

Guests board at Jetty No. 2, Princess Ferry Wharf (Bhaucha Dhakka), Mazgaon, Mumbai 400010, near Dockyard Road station on the Harbour Line. Lady Alice then heads to quieter waters on the outskirts of the harbour. See the boarding guide.
