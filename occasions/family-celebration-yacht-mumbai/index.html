Family Celebrations

Family Celebrations on a Private Yacht in Mumbai

Reunions, parents' anniversaries, homecomings and festivals: bring every generation together on the water for a day they will talk about for years.

There is space for up to 100 guests and a team that plans the food and setup with you.

Check available dates Ask on WhatsApp

What you get

  • Up to 100 guests, from grandparents to kids
  • Day or evening charters for 3, 5 or 6 hours
  • Catering planned around your family's preferences
  • The whole yacht reserved for your family

Timings

Families often choose a 5-hour charter; take the evening for sunset and city lights. Charters run in a day slot (6 am – 2 pm) or an evening slot (2 pm – 11 pm), for 3, 5 or 6 hours. 3 hours: 1 hr sailing · 2 hrs at anchor · 5 hours: 1.5 hrs sailing · 3.5 hrs at anchor · 6 hours: 3 hrs sailing · 3 hrs at anchor.

Every event is different, so we plan each charter personally. Send your request and our team confirms the details with you on WhatsApp.

Where you board

Guests board at Jetty No. 2, Princess Ferry Wharf (Bhaucha Dhakka), Mazgaon, Mumbai 400010, near Dockyard Road station on the Harbour Line. Lady Alice then heads to quieter waters on the outskirts of the harbour. See the boarding guide.
