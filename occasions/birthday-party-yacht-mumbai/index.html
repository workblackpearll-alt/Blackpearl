Birthdays

Birthday Party on a Private Yacht in Mumbai

Turn a milestone birthday into a night at sea. Lady Alice hosts up to 100 guests across her open decks, with the whole yacht reserved for your party alone.

Bring the cake, the playlist and the people. Our team handles catering, decor and the timeline so you can enjoy the evening with your guests.

Check available dates Ask on WhatsApp

What you get

  • Room for up to 100 guests, from close friends to the whole family
  • Sound and a DJ console on deck for the party
  • Catering from our partner kitchens, or your own caterer by arrangement
  • Day or evening slots, for 3, 5 or 6 hours

Timings and pricing

Most birthday parties choose a 5-hour evening charter: sunset, dinner and dancing. Charters run in a day slot (6 am – 2 pm) or an evening slot (2 pm – 11 pm), for 3, 5 or 6 hours. 3 hours: 1 hr sailing · 2 hrs at anchor · 5 hours: 1.5 hrs sailing · 3.5 hrs at anchor · 6 hours: 3 hrs sailing · 3 hrs at anchor.

Every event is different, so we quote each charter personally. Send your request and our team shares pricing for your date, slot and plans on WhatsApp.

Where you board

Guests board at Jetty No. 2, Princess Ferry Wharf (Bhaucha Dhakka), Mazgaon, Mumbai 400010, near Dockyard Road station on the Harbour Line. Lady Alice then heads to quieter waters on the outskirts of the harbour. See the boarding guide.
