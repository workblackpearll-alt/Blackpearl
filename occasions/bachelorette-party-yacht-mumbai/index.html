Bachelor & Bachelorette

Bachelorette & Bachelor Parties on a Yacht in Mumbai

Give the bride or groom a send-off at sea. Music on deck, drinks with a view and the whole yacht for your group.

Our team helps with decor, food and the playlist so you can focus on the party.

Check available dates Ask on WhatsApp

What you get

  • The whole yacht for your group, with no strangers on board
  • Sound and a DJ console on deck
  • Decor, food and drinks arranged on request
  • Day or evening charters for 3, 5 or 6 hours

Timings and pricing

Most groups choose a 3-hour evening charter; take 5 hours for a full party night. Charters run in a day slot (6 am – 2 pm) or an evening slot (2 pm – 11 pm), for 3, 5 or 6 hours. 3 hours: 1 hr sailing · 2 hrs at anchor · 5 hours: 1.5 hrs sailing · 3.5 hrs at anchor · 6 hours: 3 hrs sailing · 3 hrs at anchor.

Every event is different, so we quote each charter personally. Send your request and our team shares pricing for your date, slot and plans on WhatsApp.

Where you board

Guests board at Jetty No. 2, Princess Ferry Wharf (Bhaucha Dhakka), Mazgaon, Mumbai 400010, near Dockyard Road station on the Harbour Line. Lady Alice then heads to quieter waters on the outskirts of the harbour. See the boarding guide.
