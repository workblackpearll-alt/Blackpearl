Private Parties

Private Yacht Parties in Mumbai

Cocktail evenings, DJ nights and celebrations for no reason at all. Lady Alice gives you a private party venue on the water for up to 100 guests.

The yacht is booked by one party at a time. Our team handles food, drinks and setup so you can host.

Check available dates Ask on WhatsApp

What you get

  • Up to 100 guests across open decks
  • DJ console and sound on deck
  • Bar service with our beverage packages, or your own bottles under an FL-4 permit
  • Evening charters through to 11 pm

Timings and pricing

Most parties take a 5-hour evening charter; the longest option is 6 hours. Charters run in a day slot (6 am – 2 pm) or an evening slot (2 pm – 11 pm), for 3, 5 or 6 hours. 3 hours: 1 hr sailing · 2 hrs at anchor · 5 hours: 1.5 hrs sailing · 3.5 hrs at anchor · 6 hours: 3 hrs sailing · 3 hrs at anchor.

Every event is different, so we quote each charter personally. Send your request and our team shares pricing for your date, slot and plans on WhatsApp.

Where you board

Guests board at Jetty No. 2, Princess Ferry Wharf (Bhaucha Dhakka), Mazgaon, Mumbai 400010, near Dockyard Road station on the Harbour Line. Lady Alice then heads to quieter waters on the outskirts of the harbour. See the boarding guide.
