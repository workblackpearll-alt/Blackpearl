Pre-Wedding & Shoots

Pre-Wedding & Photo Shoots on a Yacht in Mumbai

Shoot on open decks, the bow and a styled master suite, with the sea and Mumbai's skyline as your backdrop.

Book the morning for soft light or the evening for golden hour and the city at night. The yacht is yours alone for the shoot.

Check available dates Ask on WhatsApp

What you get

  • Bow, open decks, lounge deck and a master suite to shoot in
  • Golden-hour light on the evening slot, soft light in the morning
  • Room for your photographer, stylist and crew
  • No other guests on board during your charter

Timings

A 3-hour charter usually covers a pre-wedding shoot; pick the evening for sunset frames. Charters run in a day slot (6 am – 2 pm) or an evening slot (2 pm – 11 pm), for 3, 5 or 6 hours. 3 hours: 1 hr sailing · 2 hrs at anchor · 5 hours: 1.5 hrs sailing · 3.5 hrs at anchor · 6 hours: 3 hrs sailing · 3 hrs at anchor.

Every event is different, so we plan each charter personally. Send your request and our team confirms the details with you on WhatsApp.

Where you board

Guests board at Jetty No. 2, Princess Ferry Wharf (Bhaucha Dhakka), Mazgaon, Mumbai 400010, near Dockyard Road station on the Harbour Line. Lady Alice then heads to quieter waters on the outskirts of the harbour. See the boarding guide.
