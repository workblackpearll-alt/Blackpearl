Harbour Sailing

Private Sailing Cruises Around Mumbai Harbour

Sail out of Princess Ferry Wharf into the quieter waters on the outskirts of Mumbai harbour, then drop anchor and enjoy the view.

Every charter mixes time sailing with time at anchor, and the yacht is yours alone for the trip.

Check available dates Ask on WhatsApp

What you get

  • A 3, 5 or 6-hour charter with sailing and anchored time
  • Morning cruises from 6 am or evening cruises until 11 pm
  • Up to 100 guests on board
  • Boarding at Jetty No. 2, Princess Ferry Wharf

Timings

A 3-hour evening cruise covers sunset; the 6-hour charter splits evenly between sailing and anchor. Charters run in a day slot (6 am – 2 pm) or an evening slot (2 pm – 11 pm), for 3, 5 or 6 hours. 3 hours: 1 hr sailing · 2 hrs at anchor · 5 hours: 1.5 hrs sailing · 3.5 hrs at anchor · 6 hours: 3 hrs sailing · 3 hrs at anchor.

Every event is different, so we plan each charter personally. Send your request and our team confirms the details with you on WhatsApp.

Where you board

Guests board at Jetty No. 2, Princess Ferry Wharf (Bhaucha Dhakka), Mazgaon, Mumbai 400010, near Dockyard Road station on the Harbour Line. Lady Alice then heads to quieter waters on the outskirts of the harbour. See the boarding guide.

Questions

A 3-hour charter has 1 hour sailing and 2 at anchor, 5 hours has 1.5 sailing and 3.5 at anchor, and 6 hours has 3 of each.
Lady Alice heads out to quieter waters on the outskirts of the harbour, away from the busy jetties.
Venue hours at sea are 6 am to 11 pm, with a day slot (6 am – 2 pm) and an evening slot (2 pm – 11 pm).
