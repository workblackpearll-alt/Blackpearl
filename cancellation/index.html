1. Purpose and Scope

Because Lady Alice hosts only one private party per calendar evening, confirming a date reserves the entire vessel exclusively and turns away all other prospective bookings.

This policy outlines the equitable terms governing reservation cancellations, date rescheduling, and monetary refunds.

2. 48-Hour Token Hold Money-Back Guarantee

The INR 5,000 reservation token paid on our website is 100% refundable if you decide not to proceed with the charter within 48 hours of placing the hold.

During this 48-hour consultation period, our charter team reviews menus, layout, and custom itineraries with you. If you choose not to proceed, inform us in writing or via WhatsApp, and the ₹5,000 deposit is promptly reversed to your source account.

3. Confirmed Booking Cancellation Slabs

Once a charter contract is executed and the initial 50% booking deposit is reconciled, the following terms apply for client-initiated cancellations:

  • Cancellation more than 14 days prior to departure: The charterer receives an eighty percent (80%) refund of the total deposit amount paid.
  • Cancellation between 7 to 14 days prior to departure: The charterer receives a fifty percent (50%) refund of the total deposit amount paid.
  • Cancellation less than 7 days prior to departure: Due to catering, staffing, and port commitments, cancellations within 7 days are non-refundable. However, the charterer may request a one-time date reschedule subject to calendar availability.

4. Weather Protections and Harbour Closures

Lady Alice operates year-round with protected lounge decks and indoor staterooms. However, maritime safety is non-negotiable.

If the Directorate General of Shipping, Mumbai Port Trust, or the Indian Coast Guard officially closes Mumbai Harbour to passenger navigation due to cyclonic alerts or severe squalls, the charterer receives a choice of complimentary date rescheduling within 12 months or a full 100% refund credit.

5. Technical Vessel Unavailability

If Lady Alice is rendered unavailable due to unforeseen mechanical or statutory inspection requirements, Black Pearl will immediately notify the charterer and provide an immediate 100% refund or an upgraded alternative date with complimentary catering credits.

6. Client Reschedule Requests

If the charterer provides written notice at least 7 days prior to the scheduled charter date, Black Pearl permits one (1) complimentary date reschedule within 6 months, subject to calendar slot availability.

7. Refund Processing Turnaround & Razorpay

All refunds are processed strictly through the original mode of payment via the Razorpay payment gateway infrastructure.

  • Approved refund requests are initiated by our accounts desk within 24 business hours of confirmation.
  • Banks and card networks typically reflect the credited funds within 5 to 7 working business days.
