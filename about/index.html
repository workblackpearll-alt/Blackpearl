The Philosophy

Mumbai Celebrates On Land. We Take It Further.

In a city defined by energy, celebrations often remain crowded. Hotel ballrooms, rooftop clubs, and restaurants require you to share corridors, elevators, and views with complete strangers.

Black Pearl was created to offer an alternative: an expansive, private maritime venue on the open waters of Mumbai harbour. When you step on board, there are no neighboring tables, no outside crowds, and no strangers in your memories.

"Your hundred guests. Two open decks. The whole ship yours alone for the evening."

Maritime Discipline

Captained With Care, Hosted With Discretion.

Hospitality at sea is only as good as the seamanship behind it. Lady Alice is commanded by licensed Master Captains with decades of experience navigating Mumbai Harbour and the Arabian Sea.

On every charter, our certified marine engineers monitor tides, currents, and weather radar in real time. Our on-board hospitality crew includes dedicated stewards, bartenders, and deckhands focused exclusively on your event.

We maintain strict non-disclosure and privacy protocols for high-profile clients, corporate leadership, and private families.
