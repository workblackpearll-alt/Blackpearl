The Boarding Procedure

How your party arrives, transfers, and steps aboard Lady Alice. Preview vessel spaces in our Photo Gallery or read about our vessel on About Us.

STEP 01

Arrival & Parking

Arrive 30 minutes before your slot at Princess Ferry Wharf (Bhaucha Dhakka), Mazgaon. Our team shares directions and parking details on WhatsApp.

STEP 02

Wharf Access

Proceed to Jetty No. 2. Our ground steward meets your organizer with the verified guest list.

STEP 03

Boarding

Our crew guides your party from Jetty No. 2 on board Lady Alice.

STEP 04

Gangway Welcome

Step aboard to welcome refreshments as the captain weighs anchor and the private charter begins.
