The Gateway Boarding Procedure

How your party arrives, transfers, and steps aboard Lady Alice. Preview vessel spaces in our Photo Gallery or read about our vessel on About Us.

STEP 01

Arrival & Parking

Arrive 30 minutes before your slot. Valet parking is accessible at The Taj Mahal Palace or secure parking near Regal Cinema.

STEP 02

Promenade Access

Proceed to Apollo Bunder. Our ground steward meets your organizer with verified guest manifest passes.

STEP 03

Tender Launch

Board our private tender boat at Jetty 5 for a brief 4-minute scenic harbour ride directly alongside Lady Alice.

STEP 04

Gangway Welcome

Step aboard to welcome refreshments as the captain weighs anchor and the private charter begins.
