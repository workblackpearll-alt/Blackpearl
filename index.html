Everything you need to know.

Clear, honest answers about safety, weather, catering, and booking.

Yes, absolutely. We strictly take only one booking at a time. There are no next tables, no other groups, and no strangers in your photographs. You and your guests have exclusive access to the open decks, both master suites, and all amenities with our dedicated crew of 5 to 6 looking after only you.

Guests board from the jetty at the Gateway of India, Apollo Bandar, Colaba, Mumbai 400001. The exact jetty gate number, meeting point, and boarding time are shared with every confirmed booking. Our hospitality coordinator greets you right at the entrance.

Yes, you have complete flexibility. You can choose to keep Lady Alice peacefully paused at anchor in Mumbai Harbour facing the Gateway of India—ideal for seated dining, photo shoots, and celebrations at our base rate. Alternatively, you can choose active sailing underway, where the ship cruises across Mumbai Harbour, naval waterways, and coastal horizons under power. Active sailing has a different price to cover vessel propulsion, marine navigation crew, and harbour movement clearances.

Nobody wants to pay ₹1.5 or 2 lakhs to a website on day one. A ₹5,000 token is small enough to hold your date from your phone without second thoughts, but ensures your date is blocked exclusively for you. The entire ₹5,000 is adjusted against your final invoice, and refunded in full if we cannot match your requested schedule.

Safety is non-negotiable. Our vessel operations strictly follow port authority regulations. If maritime authorities issue weather warnings or sea conditions are unsafe to cruise, you are offered an immediate reschedule to any available date of your choice or a 100% full refund of your hold deposit.

We offer bespoke floral arches, fairy light draping, sound systems, DJ setups, and gourmet catering ranging from finger appetizers to five-course seated banquets. You may also bring in your preferred decorators or caterers with prior coordination.

Lady Alice features two full, air-conditioned master staterooms with private ensuite bathrooms. They serve as comfortable private dressing suites for hosts, staging rooms for artists or DJs, and peaceful resting spots for grandparents or children during longer celebrations.
